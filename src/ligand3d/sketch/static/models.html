Every minimization method ligand3d knows about, what it costs, and whether it can run on this machine right now. The column that matters most is charge: explicit means the method consumes the total molecular charge as an input; implicit means it sees only atoms and positions, so a carboxylate and a neutral acid that lost a proton look identical to it. ligand3d refuses the second pairing rather than answering it.
